## 👋 About Me

London-based **Data Scientist and Python Developer**, formally trained through **Imperial College London** (96% average). I work across the full data workflow — from raw data through to deployed model — building clean, well-documented solutions grounded in statistical rigour.

Alongside data science, I independently design, build, and ship — Android apps, trader tools, generative art systems, live wallpapers, screensavers, interactive maps, and word-cloud typography. Thirty-four projects across the **Google Play Store**, **Gumroad**, **Lemon Squeezy**, and **Envato**. This side of my work has sharpened real product instincts: pricing, positioning, knowing when to ship and when to keep going — those lessons sit alongside the code.

I'm available for **freelance and contract work**, open to collaboration, and actively seeking a data or Python development role.
